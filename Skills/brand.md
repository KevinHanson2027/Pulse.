# 1. Brand Core

Pulse is the performance-driven social operating system for real life.

It is a platform where discipline becomes social currency.

---

### Mission

Turn discipline into identity through high-agency social interaction.

### Vision

Become the global infrastructure for those who refuse to be average.

### Brand Promise

We don’t track your life.  
We optimize your execution.

### Emotional Positioning

Confident. High-agency. Strategically intense.

### Identity Statement

Pulse is the operating system for the disciplined.

---

# 2. Target Identity

Pulse is for the high-agency individual who rejects the softness of modern wellness tools.

---

### Psychological Profile

- Growth-obsessed
- Competitive
- Data-reliant
- System-oriented

Life is viewed as something to optimize.

---

### Behavioral Profile

- Habit-driven
- Metric-focused
- Public but authentic
- Performance-centered

---

### Values

- Momentum
- Visibility
- Raw data
- Real-world activation

---

### Rejects

- Pastel productivity
- Bubbly UI
- Instagram aesthetics
- Fluffy wellness language

---

### Motivation Drivers

- Streak pressure (Duolingo energy)
- Performance comparison (Strava energy)
- Verified authenticity (BeReal energy)

---

### Language Style

- Direct
- Verb-first
- Metric-heavy

---

# 3. Tone of Voice

Clear. Sharp. Strategic. No fluff.

---

### Sentence Structure

- Short
- Punchy
- Declarative

---

### Energy Level

Controlled intensity.  
High-agency.

---

### Word Rules

**Use:**

- Execute
- Momentum
- Verified
- Output
- Peak
- Raw
- Chain
- Metric

**Never Use:**

- Journey
- Soft
- Gentle
- Maybe
- Feel-good
- Self-care
- Explore

---

### Copy Examples

**Dashboard**

> Output: Peak.

**Streak Notification**

> Hold the line. Day 45.

**Leaderboard**

> Climb or fall.

**Empty State**

> Zero data. Start moving.

**Error Message**

> Execution failed. Retry.

**Push Notification**

> Pressure is a privilege. Log your PR.

# 4. Messaging Pillars

Pulse communicates through four core values.

---

## Discipline

Consistency over motivation.

**Copy**

> 05:00 AM. Execution starts now.

**UI**

- Bold streak counts
- High-contrast counters

---

## Visibility

If there is no proof, it didn’t happen.

**Copy**

> Verified PR. Proof attached.

**UI**

- Video proof attached to milestones

---

## Momentum

Consecutive wins compound.

**Copy**

> Don’t break the chain.

**UI**

- Velocity-based trendlines
- Momentum indicators

---

## Accountability

Social proof as performance fuel.

**Copy**

> The community is watching. Execute.

**UI**

- Public streak visibility
- Leaderboard positioning

# 7. Visual Philosophy

Minimal. Powerful. No clutter.

---

### Layout Density

High-information density.  
Metrics first.

---

### Whitespace

Strategic.  
Used to isolate primary metrics.

---

### Iconography

- Sharp lines
- Minimum 2px stroke
- No rounded or bubbly forms

---

### Motion

- Fast
- Snappy
- Framer Motion
- No dreamy fades

---

### Image / Video Style

- Raw
- Grainy
- Harsh lighting
- No filters

---

### Typography Hierarchy

**Headings:** Sora (Bold)  
**Body:** Inter (Clean)

---

### Color Palette

- Charcoal — #111111
- Ice Blue — #4DA6FF
- Soft Grey — #F5F5F5

---

# 8. Do’s and Don’ts

---

## Do

- Use strong active verbs
- Show raw metrics
- Celebrate long-term consistency
- Enforce streak pressure

---

## Don’t

- Use pastel gradients
- Use fluffy wellness language
- Overexplain features
- Allow aesthetic-filter uploads
